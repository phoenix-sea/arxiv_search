I work on search and ranking systems and follow LLM research that affects them.

Core interests (score these highest):
- Information retrieval: dense, sparse and hybrid retrieval; embedding models; semantic search
- Ranking: learning to rank, rerankers (cross-encoders, LLM-based listwise/pointwise rerankers), ranking distillation
- LLMs applied to search: query understanding and rewriting, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), generative retrieval, relevance judgement with LLMs
- Recommender systems that use LLMs or modern ranking models
- Production / industrial-scale search and ranking: serving efficiency, latency, inference optimisation for ranking models, online A/B results
- Evaluation of retrieval, ranking and RAG systems; benchmarks and datasets

Also interesting (medium):
- General LLM advances likely to matter for retrieval or ranking: long context, efficient inference, small/distilled models, fine-tuning methods, agents that use search tools

Not interesting (low):
- Vision-only, robotics, speech, theory without retrieval/ranking implications, domain applications with no new retrieval or ranking method
