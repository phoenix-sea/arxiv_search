Search syntax

Words are ANDed: dense retrieval distillation. Phrases: "learning to rank". Operators: LLM AND (reranker OR reranking) NOT survey. Prefix: rerank*. Field: title: "semantic search", authors: Borisyuk. Stemming is on (ranking ≈ ranked).

Author takes a full name or a surname. Middle names are optional and initials match full names, so Samuel Fraiberger also finds "Samuel P. Fraiberger" and S Fraiberger also finds "Samuel Fraiberger". Organization matches company, university, lab or email domain from the paper's author block (World Bank, worldbankgroup.org, Development Impact AI Lab). Click any author or organization to search for it.
